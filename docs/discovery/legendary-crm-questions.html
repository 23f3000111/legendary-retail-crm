Before we build

A few questions about how Legendary really works

You have seen the demo, so you know the shape of the system. Now we need to understand how your business actually runs day to day, so the real system fits the way you work instead of making you change to fit it.

There are 96 questions. Most take one line to answer. If you are not sure about something, just write “not sure yet” — that is genuinely helpful, because it tells us what is still to be decided.

Customers and where they come from

Best answered by Vincent Lim, with whoever handles marketing

27
Answer first

Do you want to keep a record of each customer — their name, contact, what they bought, when they came back? Or do you only want to count how many customers came from each country?

Why we ask: this decides what we are actually building for you. Counting gives you very good reports, and that is what the demo showed. Keeping records of each person is a different thing: you could see who buys again, run a membership scheme, and send offers. That is a much bigger system, and it brings legal duties about looking after people’s personal details. Both are perfectly good choices — we just need to know which one you want, because it is difficult to switch later.

28

How does a staff member know where a customer is from — do they ask, see the passport at tax refund, or judge it from the language spoken?

29
Answer first

Do you need to know exactly which nationality bought which perfume? Or is the daily total by country good enough?

Why we ask: at the moment the system knows what was sold, and separately who walked in, and works out a sensible estimate from the two. To be exact, staff would have to record the customer’s country on every single sale, one at a time. Worth thinking honestly about whether a busy counter on a Saturday would really do that.

What happens next

Once we have the eight priority answers, we will write up a plain plan and draw every screen for you to look at and approve. You will also get a proper build schedule and a firm price at that point.

Nothing gets built until you have approved that. And wherever an answer is still unknown, we will write down the assumption we are working to, so nothing important gets decided quietly on your behalf.
