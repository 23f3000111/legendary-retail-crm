# A few questions about how Legendary really works

**Fill-in version** — please type your answer under each question.

You have seen the demo, so you know the shape of the system. Now we need to understand how your
business actually runs day to day, so the real system fits the way you work instead of making
you change to fit it.

There are 96 questions. Most take one line to answer. If you are not sure about something, just
write **"not sure yet"** — that is genuinely helpful, because it tells us what is still to be
decided.

## 4 · Customers and where they come from
*Best answered by Vincent Lim, with whoever handles marketing*

**27.** ⚠️ **ANSWER FIRST** — Do you want to keep a record of each customer — their name, contact, what they bought, when they came back? Or do you only want to count how many customers came from each country?
*Why we ask: this decides what we are actually building for you. Counting gives you very good reports, and that is what the demo showed. Keeping records of each person is a different thing: you could see who buys again, run a membership scheme, and send offers. That is a much bigger system, and it brings legal duties about looking after people's personal details. Both are perfectly good choices — but it is difficult to switch later.*
>

**28.** How does a staff member know where a customer is from — do they ask, see the passport at tax refund, or judge it from the language spoken?
>

**29.** ⚠️ **ANSWER FIRST** — Do you need to know exactly which nationality bought which perfume? Or is the daily total by country good enough?
*Why we ask: at the moment the system knows what was sold, and separately who walked in, and works out a sensible estimate from the two. To be exact, staff would have to record the customer's country on every single sale. Worth thinking honestly about whether a busy counter on a Saturday would really do that.*
>

**30.** Are your airport shops part of the tourist tax refund scheme? Does that create paperwork this system should produce or keep?
>

**31.** Do you have a membership or loyalty scheme today, or would you like one? How would it work?
>

**32.** Would you want to contact customers afterwards by email or WhatsApp? Do you ask their permission today, and how?
>

**33.** Would you like to record which staff member served a customer, or how the customer heard about you?
>

**34.** Should the system recognise a returning customer across different shops, or is each shop separate?
>

## 5 · Stock and how it moves

## What happens next

Once we have the eight priority answers, we will write up a plain plan and draw every screen for
you to look at and approve. You will also get a proper build schedule and a firm price at that
point.

Nothing gets built until you have approved that. And wherever an answer is still unknown, we
will write down the assumption we are working to, so nothing important gets decided quietly on
your behalf.
