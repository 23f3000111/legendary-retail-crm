# Revision 2 — 9 September 2026

Built from `CRM Revision 2.pdf`, the re-sent store list, and four further notes
from the client. This is what changed, and the seven things that still need an
answer.

---

## The big one: sign-in

**PIN removed. Username, password, and a six-digit code sent to the person's
work e-mail.**

This is also the end of the one compromise that had been flagged at every
hand-over. The PIN had to be *readable*, because senior staff wanted to look one
up — which meant keeping a recoverable copy behind an encryption key. A password
does not: it is stored as a one-way hash and nobody can read it back, including
Davy and IT. The whole "who may see whose credential" question disappears,
because the answer is now nobody.

The authority table survives unchanged — who may *reset* whose is exactly who
could change whose PIN. Full write-up in [`auth.md`](./auth.md).

## Close the day

**Four steps became three.** Sales and the stock count were separate, which meant
a promoter read the same product twice on one screen — once for what sold and
again for what to count. They are one table now:

| Product | Sold today | Should be | Counted | Difference |

That is the client's *"the product bought need to be done 1 time not 2 times"*.
It also settles the item held over from the last round: Revision 2's screenshots
still show the stepped wizard, so the wizard stays — it is just shorter.

**The top-up is no longer a guess.** It used to list only what the app thought
the shop needed, which is a guess dressed up as a decision — the reorder points
are the client's own numbers and will not be right until there is real data
behind them (Q37). A promoter can now add **any** line, testers included, and
take any line off. What is low is still filled in for them.

## The phone

Order lines could not be swiped sideways on a phone, and the client was right
that it was unusable. Rather than fix the swipe, the table now becomes **a card
per line below `sm`** — five columns on a 390px screen is the wrong shape even
when the swipe works, and there is now nothing to swipe.
