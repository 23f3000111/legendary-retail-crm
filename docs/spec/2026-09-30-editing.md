# Changing what is already in — 30 September 2026

**Asked on 29 September:** anything put into the CRM should be editable, and
editing should be simple. The example was a closing with a mistake in it:
putting it right meant filling in the whole day again.

**What changed:** every record a person enters now has a **Change** button.
It opens the same screen the record was entered on, already filled in with
what is on record. You change the one thing that is wrong and save. Every
change is written to the activity log as a before → after.

---

## What can be changed, by whom

| Record | Who | Where |
|---|---|---|
| **A sale** | The promoter at that outlet — today, or any day not yet closed | *Record a sale* → **Change** on the sale. Change the item, the price (Promotion, Retail, Offer, Other), the quantity or the customer's country; add an item or take one off. *Delete sale* is still there for a sale that should not exist at all. |
| **Today's closing** | The promoter at that outlet, until midnight | *Today* → **Change the closing**. Opens with the cash, the card and every shelf count as filed. Saved at once. |
| **An earlier day's closing** (1 to 3 days back) | The promoter asks; **Kelly or Davy approve** | *History* → **Ask to correct**. The same form, filled in; the promoter changes what was wrong and says why. Nothing on the closing moves until Kelly or Davy approves. The request can be changed, or taken back, while it waits. |
| **Any closing, any day** | Kelly or Davy | *Closings* → tap the day in the grid, or a store's page → the day → **Change this closing**. Saved at once. |
| **An order waiting for Kelly** | The promoter at that outlet, or Kelly/Davy | The order → **Change order**: quantities, items, urgent or not, the note. |
| **An order Kelly turned down** | The promoter at that outlet | The order → **Change and send again**. It goes back to Kelly. |
| **An approved order** | Kelly trims at approval (as before); the warehouse packs short | *Mark packed* now asks what actually went, each box starting at what Kelly approved. |
| Targets, promotions, logins | As before | Already editable. |
| **The activity log** | Nobody | By design. It is the record of every change above. |

The three-day window and Kelly's approval are the client's own rule from the
discovery form (Q19). Nothing else about who may do what has changed.

---

## Why correcting used to mean filling the day in again

*File it again* re-opened the closing form as if the day had never been
filed:

- **Every shelf count came back wrong.** The boxes start at "what the shelf
  should hold": the last count less the day's sales. The last count was the
  day's own closing — which already had the day's sales taken off — so they
  were taken off twice, and every box was short.
- **The cash came back empty.**
- **The top-up order was offered again**, so re-filing could send Kelly a
  second order.

Now changing a closing opens it exactly as filed, and the shelf is counted
from the closing *before* that day. A missed day filed late gets the same fix.

---

## When a sale changes after the day is closed

The closing is not changed behind the promoter's back — the cash they counted
is theirs to confirm. Instead *Today* and *Record a sale* say:

> The sales have changed since today was closed. **Update the closing**

The button opens the closing with the new sales in, the cash as filed and the
card worked out again, and lists what changed. One tap to save.

---

## What Kelly sees

A correction waiting for her shows, line by line, what approving it would
change — the same sentences the promoter saw before sending it, and the same
ones the activity log keeps:

> Sales RM 3,596 → RM 3,408 (22 → 21 units) · Orchid · 30ml: 2 → 1 ·
> Cash RM 637 → RM 449

She can approve it, or turn it down with a note the store sees on its
history — from the Closings screen, or from the day itself.

## Held by the server, not just the screens

`put_docs` and `remove_docs` in [`0001_init.sql`](../../supabase/migrations/0001_init.sql)
now refuse, whoever sends the request:

- a promoter changing an earlier day's closing outright — they may only ask;
- anyone but Kelly or Davy approving or turning down a correction;
- a promoter undoing or rewriting a correction that has been decided;
- a request more than three days after the day;
- anyone but Kelly or Davy deleting a filed day.

`scripts/verify-server.mjs` checks each of these against the real database, in
a transaction it rolls back. The local backend keeps the same rules
(`closingWriteRefusal` in `src/lib/editing.ts`), so the tests exercise them too.

## Not yet changeable from the screens

- **Products and prices, and the outlet list.** These are set in the code, by
  us. The *Add a product* button on the Products screen does not do anything
  yet. The open question on the travel kits' retail price is one example of
  something the client could set themselves if this were built.
