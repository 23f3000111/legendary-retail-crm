# Revision 8 — 20 October 2026

The client's document is headed "Revision #6"; this is the eighth round of
changes on our side. It has six requests across four pages: the promoter's
order, Finance's report, the store page's customers, and the product list.
All six are built. Three questions went back before building, and the
client's answers are below.

The document is in `docs/client-input/CRM Revision 8.pdf`. Like the other
client files, it is kept out of git.

## The three answers

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| "Draft PO even without closing": send straight away, or keep a draft? | **Both.** A promoter can save the order as a draft, which only their store sees, and send it later. Or they can send it straight away. |
| What is one row of Finance's report? | **One store on one business date**, with its units and revenue. The export gives the date on every row. |
| Adding and removing products: may Davy, Kelly and Chloe change prices too? | **Yes.** They can add products, change prices, reorder levels and case sizes, and take products off. |

## What was asked, and what changed

### The promoter's order

| # | Asked for | What changed |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Make the order lines draggable, to arrange their sequence | Every line on an order has a handle (⠿) on its left. Drag a line by the handle to move it up or down; this works with a finger on a phone or tablet, and with a mouse. The order is sent in that sequence, and its page lists the lines that way, for Kelly and the warehouse too. This applies when closing the day, on a new order, and when changing an order. On a keyboard, the handle also answers the up and down arrow keys. If an order is changed only by moving lines, the log records "Changed the order of the items". |
| 2 | A draft PO, without having to close the day | *My orders* and *Today* now have a **New order** button. It opens the same order the closing builds: anything low is already on it, and the promoter can add, take off and drag lines, mark it urgent and write a note for Kelly. Then they choose one of two buttons. **Send to Kelly** sends it now, exactly as a closing would. **Save as draft** keeps it at the store. |

**Drafts.** A draft appears under the new **Drafts** tab on *My orders*, and
at the top of *Today*'s orders. Its page shows three buttons: **Change
draft**, **Delete draft**, and **Send to HQ**.

Head office does not see a draft. It is not in Kelly's queue, in the orders
in flight, in the overview's supply panel, or on the store's page. An order
reaches head office when it is sent. From then on it counts as raised on the
day it was sent, not the day the draft was first saved.

When a store closes the day while it has a draft, the closing's order step
reminds the promoter to send it.

Only a draft can be deleted. An order that has been sent cannot be deleted,
and the server enforces this as well. Before this change the server did not
stop anyone deleting an order. The app never offered it, but nothing on the
server prevented it.

### Products: Davy, Kelly and Chloe

| # | Asked for | What changed |
|---|---|---|
| 7 | Let the three of us add and remove products | Davy and Kelly now have *Products* under *More*. Chloe already had it on her bar. **Add a product** puts a new line on the range: a new size or kit of a fragrance already on it, or the first line of a new fragrance in one of the four collections. A bottle, set or travel kit needs a promotion and a retail price. A **Promotion 2** price is filled in as RM 198 when the promotion price is RM 188, and can be changed. A line can also be marked as one that may be given free (FOC), like the Wishes. A vial has no price and is always given free. A tester has no price and is never sold. **What "taken off" means.** A product that is taken off leaves *Record a
sale*, the nightly count and the order list, at every store. Every sale,
count and order already made still shows it, and the report can still pick
it. It is listed at the foot of *Products* under **Taken off**, with a
**Put back** button. A product is never deleted: the server refuses it,
because past sales name it.

**A new product.** A new product goes on *Record a sale* and the order list
straight away. At each store's next closing it is counted like any product
new to the shelf. Its box starts empty, and the day cannot be closed until
it is counted.

**Changing a price.** A new price applies to sales from then on. Sales
already made keep the price they went at.

**The log.** Every change is logged under *Products* with the old and new
values, for example "Changed Nyonya · Travel Kit — Promotion RM 68 → RM 78".

The Products screen lets the client set the prices still open from
Revision 6 themselves. Those are the Nyonya Travel Kit and the Spirit 1 Set
(new).

## On the server

The server accepts product records only from Davy, Kelly and Chloe. It also
checks each record, whatever app or device sent it:

- the record matches its own line
- it is a kind of product the range has: bottle, set, travel kit, vial or tester
- it has a fragrance, a size and a code
- every price is 0 or more, and a product that is sold has both prices
- the reorder level and case size are whole numbers

The name on each record is that of whoever is signed in. The server refuses
to delete a product, and refuses to delete any order that is not a draft.

The server now also enforces the steps an order goes through, for every
write. An order is created as a draft or is sent straight away. After that,
each desk can make only its own move: a store sends its draft, or sends an
order again after Kelly has turned it down; Kelly or Davy approve or reject
it; the warehouse packs and dispatches it; and the store or Kelly confirms it
arrived. A change that keeps the order at the same step still goes through,
such as an edit or Finance's clearance.

Before this, a store could have marked its own order approved, or turned a
sent order back into a draft and then deleted it, by writing to the server
without going through the app. The app never offered either. The automated
security review found the second one.

`scripts/verify-server.mjs` checks all of this on the live server, inside a
transaction that is rolled back:

- Kelly can add a product.
- Finance and a promoter cannot.
- A price below 0 is refused.
- The product is signed with Kelly's name.
- A product cannot be deleted.
- A store can delete its own draft, but not an order it has sent.
- A store cannot turn a sent order back into a draft, or approve its own order.
- Kelly can approve it.
