## Page by page

### Finance: Stores

The Stores page opens on the stores themselves: no four boxes and no sales
report. The table comes first, then the calendar, then the stores opening
soon.

### Finance: a store's page

Only the calendar and the day chosen on it — "for finance they only want
detail, there is no summary needed".

1. **The calendar**, at the top. Tap a day to choose it. It starts on the
   last day the store filed.
2. **The day**, under it:
   - **Sales, Cash, Card and Customers**, cash and card as filed at the close.
   - **Stock**: every product on the shelf, in the client's order, with the
     price it went for that day, **Deducted** (what the day took off the
     shelf: sold, plus any tester, damage or sample) and **On hand at close**
     (the count filed that day). **Days left and Status are gone.** Today
     shows its sales so far; the shelf is counted when the day closes. A day
     that was not filed says so.
   - **Where the customers came from**, that day, every country.
   - **CSV** and **PDF report** of the day in full.

There is no "equal", no item and no nationality rule.

### Analytics

One choice of dates drives the whole page: **7, 30 or 90 days, this month,
last month, or Dates** (From and To). Only those dates are shown:

- **Sales** (against the same number of days before), **Units sold**,
  **Customers** and **Stores that sold**: the four windows are gone.
- **Every country**, ten at a time, none folded into "Other" (the ribbon
  still groups the small ones).
- **Products**: each product's total for the dates, in the client's order.
  **Chart** ranks the best twelve.
- **By store**: each store's total for the dates, with its customers and
  against the days before.
- The trend and the trading pattern, at the foot.

The separate sales report is gone from the page: the filter's dates,
stores, products and nationalities do its work.

### The exports

The CSV and PDF on a store's day and on Analytics are one detailed report,
for the dates and filter set:

1. **Sales by store**, where there is more than one.
2. **Sales by product**: each product, every date it sold, at each price,
   with its units and net sales, a **subtotal under each product**, and no
   rows for what did not sell.
3. The dealers' and consignment partners' months keyed in, where nothing
   narrows by product or country.
4. **Payment**: cash, card (and e-wallet) as filed at each close.
5. **Closing stock**: deducted and on hand at each close. In the PDF only
   for a single day; a range's is in the CSV.
6. **Customers** by country, summed.
