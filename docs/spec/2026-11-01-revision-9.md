# Revision 9 — 1 November 2026

The client's document is headed "Legendary CRM Amendments #7"; this is the
ninth round of changes on our side. It asks for the same four sales boxes on
every main page, figures before graphs, a total under every column of
amounts, a calendar of daily sales, a closer look at each country, and the
sales report (the "counter") on more pages. Everything in it is built. Four
questions went back before building, and the client's answers are below.

The document is in `docs/client-input/CRM Revision 9.pdf`. Like the other
client files, it is kept out of git.

## The four answers

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| The Overview's calendar: where does it go, and does Supply stay? | **The calendar takes Supply's place, and Supply stays below it.** |
| The store page's stock table: which ten come first? | **Best sellers first**: what the store sold most of over the last 30 days. |
| The CSV "looks very boring": what would be better? | **A PDF report** as well as the CSV: a designed page with boxes, a bar chart and the table with its total, to print or save as a PDF. |
| The Stores table: which columns? | **Only the four sales columns**: today, yesterday, month to date and last month. Target pace and Latest are gone. |

## The rules that apply everywhere

The client's last page sets eight rules. This is how each one now works.

| # | Rule | What changed |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Add, remove and change prices and promotions for every product | Already in place since Revision 8: *Products* and *Promotions*, for Davy, Kelly and Chloe. |
| 2 | Graphs go to the bottom, figures go up | On Analytics, each store's page and Revenue, the line charts and the trading pattern now sit at the foot of the page. |
| 3 | Pie and bar charts are secondary; figures come first | Every chart that has figures now opens on the figures. The chart is one tap away, on the **Chart** button in its corner, and **Figures** brings the table back. |
| 4 | Overview, Stores and Analytics have the same four boxes | The four boxes are on the Overview, Stores, Analytics and Closings, and on each store's own page for that store. |
| 5 | Overview, Stores and Analytics have the counter, with date, store, item and nationality | The sales report is on all three, and on Revenue and each store's page as it was. It has a new **Store** rule. |
| 6 | The Stores page is about sales | The Stores page is now the four boxes, the sales report, and a table of every store's sales. |
| 7 | Analytics stays detailed, with more prominent buttons | The period and measure buttons are full size, and **Filter by store, product, country** is a solid button. Everything that was on the page is still there. |
| 8 | A day on the calendar shows each store's sales, the products sold and where the buyers came from | A day opens a window with all three, each with its total. |

### The four boxes

| Box | What it counts |
|---|---|
| **Today sales** | Today, as it stands: every sale recorded at the counter so far. |
| **Yesterday sales** | Yesterday, as filed. Underneath, how many main stores have filed it, for example "8 of 9 stores filed". |
| **Month to date sales** | From the 1st of this month to now. |
| **Last month sales** | The whole of last month, including the dealers' and consignment partners' months keyed in on *Dealer sales*. |

Each box also gives its number of customers. The old units-sold box is gone.

On a store's page the boxes are for that store alone. On Analytics they
follow the filter's stores, products and countries, but each box keeps its
own days. A line above the boxes says when they are narrowed.

### Totals

Every table with a column of amounts now has a total at the foot of that
column:

- best sellers
- the countries
- every store
- Analytics' stores
- a country's basket
- the store page's stock (deducted and on hand)
- the Closings grid
- the consignment partners on Revenue
- the day and country windows
- the sales report

Where a table shows only some of its rows, the total says so. "Total · all 39"
on the stock table counts every product, not only the ten showing. "Top 12
total" on the Overview's best sellers adds up the twelve shown.

The CSV exports from Stores, Analytics, the stock sheet and the sales report
end with a total row.

### The PDF report

The **PDF report** button opens a designed page in a new tab. It has:

- the Legendary header
- the figures in coloured boxes
- the rules the report was drawn up with
- a bar chart
- the table with its total

The browser's print window opens with it, and **Save as PDF** there makes the
file. Nothing is sent anywhere. If the page does not open, the browser is
blocking pop-ups: allow them for this site and press the button again.

The button is on the sales report, wherever the report appears, and on the
Stores and Analytics pages.

### Customers, not bottles

Every count of nationality is now a count of customers. One sale is one
customer, however much they bought. Before, it counted the bottles sold to
each nationality. This applies to *Which countries bought*, *Where they came
from*, and the promoter's own *Today*, *Record a sale* and *History*.

A line recorded before sales were grouped has nothing to say which sale it
belonged to, so it counts as one customer.

## Page by page

### Overview

### Stores

The page is the four boxes, then the sales report, then the table.

The table has one row per store and four columns:

- **Today sales · live**
- **Yesterday sales · 31 Oct 2026**
- **Month to date sales · since 1 Nov 2026**
- **Last month sales · Oct 2026**

Each figure gives its customers underneath, and each column has its total.
The stores run from the best month so far down. Tap a store to open it.

Dealers and consignment partners report once a month, so their today,
yesterday and month to date show a dash, and last month is their keyed-in
figure.

**CSV** downloads the table with the dates in its headings and a total row.
**PDF report** makes the designed page.

### A store's page

From the top of the page down:

1. **The four boxes**, for this store.
2. **Stock**. It shows the store's best sellers first, ten at a time.
   **Load more** adds ten, and **Show all** shows the rest. The title counts
   anything out of stock or running low. The foot totals Deducted and On hand
   for every product.
3. **The calendar**, in place of *Recent closings*. Tap a day to see that
   day's total, every product sold with its units, its sales and the flags of
   the countries that bought it, and where the customers came from. Kelly and
   Davy can open the day's closing from there.
4. **The sales report**, for this store, as before.
5. **Best sellers here**. Every product sold in the period, with its units
   and its amount in MYR, and the total. The bars are behind **Chart**.
6. **Where they came from**. This counts customers. Tap a country to see
   which products it bought. The ribbon is behind **Chart**.
7. **Orders**.
8. **The revenue graph**, at the foot.

### Analytics

- **The buttons.** The period and measure buttons are full size. **Filter by
  store, product, country** is a solid button. **Clear** takes the filter
  off.
- **The four boxes**, below the filter, narrowed by it.
- **The figures**, in this order:
  - *Which countries bought*: customers, units and sales, and tap a country to open it.
  - Revenue by channel.
  - Product performance.
  - Every store, with its total. On a phone, the channel and trend columns step aside so the figures fit.
- **The sales report.**
- **The graphs**: the trend and the trading pattern, at the foot.
- **Exports.** **CSV** ends with a total row. **PDF report** draws up the period and the filter, with every store that sold.

### Closings

The four boxes now open the page. *Revenue filed yesterday* is gone, because
the Yesterday box gives that figure. The filing grid has a total row. Under
each day it shows how many stores filed, for example "8/9", in red when one
is missing. Under *Yesterday* it shows yesterday's takings.

### Revenue (Finance)

The consignment table has a total row: units, revenue to us, and how many
partners have reported. The revenue and channel charts have moved to the foot
of the page.

### The sales report

There is a new **Store** rule. It is *equal* or *not equal* to one or more
stores, chosen from the main stores, dealers and consignment partners. On a
store's own page the store is fixed, so the rule is not offered there. Next to
**CSV**, a **PDF report** button draws up the rows with a chart: by day for
one store or several days, and by store for a single day.

## Also fixed

A window opened from inside a panel, such as a day on the calendar, used to
sit inside that panel. It now always opens in the middle of the screen.

## On the server

Nothing changed on the server this round. Every figure is read from records
already there.
