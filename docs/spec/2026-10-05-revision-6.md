## The first night with the new products

The five new products in point 6 have never been counted anywhere. At each
outlet's next closing their boxes start **empty**, marked *New — type what is
on the shelf*, and the day cannot be closed until they are counted. Every
other box fills itself in as usual.

Before this, a product new to a store would have started at 0, and closing
without looking would have filed "none on the shelf" for it — and raised an
out-of-stock alert for it at every store. Now a product counts as unknown at a
store until that store has counted it: no alert, no top-up suggestion, and "—"
on the stock screens rather than 0.

Changing a closing filed before these products existed does not ask for them.
