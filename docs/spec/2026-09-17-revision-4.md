## The price a sale went for

The price list in *CRM Revision 2* gives every line two prices — retail and
promotion — and the three Wishes a third, an RM 10 offer. Until now the counter
recorded a sale at whichever price the store is *counted* on, so a customer who
paid full price at Pavilion was recorded at the promotion price, and a Wish
added on at RM 10 was recorded at RM 88.

Now each item is offered at every price it can go for:

- **Promotion** and **Retail** on everything, the store's own price first —
  promotion nearly everywhere, retail at BSAS and Sasa;
- **Offer** on Wish 1, 2 and 3 only, because only they carry one.

The basket, tonight's closing, the Activity line, and every report count the
line at the price that was tapped. Where nothing was tapped — the seeded
history, and the dealer and consignment channels, which report a figure rather
than ringing up a sale — the store's basis still applies, exactly as before.

An item without an offer price can never be sold at one; asking for a tier the
item does not have falls back to its everyday price.

This is also the first time the question *"how much did we sell at full
price?"* has an answer in the data.

## Closing on a phone

The shelf count was a four-column table with a minimum width, so on a phone the
count box sat off the right-hand edge. That was the screenshot the client sent.

The shelf is now a card per product below tablet width — the name, what sold
and what should be there on the left, the box on the right — and the table only
appears where there is room for it. The top-up lines, the product picker and
the closing button are laid out the same way, and the page is checked at 390px
with no sideways scroll.
